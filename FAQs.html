General

Do you have a physical store?

Unfortunately we don't. But we deliver all over Pakistan and internationally. You just have to order at our website and it'll be on your doorstep. Sometimes we do exhibitions in Karachi — follow our Instagram for updates.

How can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order by contacting our customer service within 24 hours of placing it.

Why is my order delayed?

Orders may be delayed due to high demand, customs, or delivery partner delays. We’ll keep you updated.

What is the mode of payment?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Bank Transfers, and various online payment options.



Product Related

Are the measurements mentioned exact for all hijabs?

All measurements for hijabs are approximate. We measure one piece of each design containing hundreds of pieces. It's not possible to measure all hundreds and thousands of pieces. Hence please allow margin of 2-3 inches for all measurements mentioned, due to manual error.

Will the colors be exactly as shown in pictures?

Although we try our best to capture colors as close as possible to how they appear in real, we cannot guarantee a hundred percent match. The difference between different display screens is minimal but beyond our control. For a test, you can view the same picture on our website in two different phones - Apple and Samsung and you'll be able to understand the difference that different LCDs cause.



Shipping and Delivery

How long does it take for delivery?

How long does it take for delivery? All orders within Karachi take from 2-4 working days for delivery, exclusive of Sundays, Public holidays, and/or any miscellaneous strikes etc. Orders outside Karachi may take 4-8 working days for delivery.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Please WhatsApp us (on +92-334-7313410) to know about shipping rates as they vary with your order weight and destination country.

Do you have return or exchange policy?

We don't have a return policy but we do have an exchange policy. You must initiate an exchange request within 3 days of receiving your order through email Please email at salam@thewomenzone.pk mentioning your order ID, reason of exchange and the items you want to send back. Once approved, please send the exchange items back at our Karachi warehouse with your original order ID mentioned clearly on top of it. The items should reach us no later than 10 days after your request has been approved. We’ll issue you a voucher of the scarves amount after inspection that you can use at any of your future purchases. For an exchange to take place, the item(s) must be in the same condition they were delivered in with all their packaging and/or tag(s) intact. Claim of exchange can be rejected if item(s) are found to be stained, worn, or modified from their original condition when they reach us, in which case the buyer will not be compensated. Customers are responsible for any courier charges incurred during the exchange process due to change of mind
