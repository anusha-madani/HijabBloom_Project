Privacy Policy – Hijab Bloom

At Hijab Bloom, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you shop with us.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Contact information (email, phone number, address)
  • Payment details (processed securely through our payment partners)
  • Order history
  • Your preferences (e.g., product choices, sizes)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate with you about your purchases, promotions, and updates
  • Improve our products and services
  • Personalize your shopping experience

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only with:

  • Payment processors (to complete transactions)
  • Courier and shipping companies (to deliver your orders)
  • IT service providers (to operate our website securely)

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and personalize recommendations. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correct or update your details
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
